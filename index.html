A farm, not a factory

Small batches. Big-hearted farming.

Dominic Nitiwul’s farm started with a few pigs, a patch of good earth and a simple idea: raising animals well means paying attention every day. We rear our pigs on the farm from the day they are born, feeding them well, keeping them healthy and letting them grow. Once they are ready, we sell them as piglets or as fully grown pigs to farmers and buyers who want well-cared-for animals.

Our family still does the feeding, the fence mending and the muddy-boot mornings. Buying from a farm you can visit means you know exactly how your pigs were raised.

Rearing on our farm

From birth to sale.

Every pig is reared on our farm. We look after them as they grow, and sell them when they are healthy and ready.

01

Born and nursed

Piglets stay warm and nurse with their mother in a clean pen.

02

Weaned

From about 4 to 8 weeks they move onto solid feed. Piglets can be sold from 8 weeks.

03

Growing up

Bigger pens, more room to roam and a growers’ ration while they gain size.

04

Fully grown

Healthy, fully grown pigs are ready for their new owners.

A few good questions

Good to know.

What do you sell?

We rear pigs on the farm and sell them when they are ready: young piglets for buyers who want to grow them on, and fully grown pigs.

Can I visit the farm?

Yes, by appointment. Send a visit request with your preferred date and group size and we’ll confirm by email.

How do I buy pigs?

Add the pigs you want to your enquiry list and email us. We’ll reply with availability, price and next steps.

Where is the farm?

We’re in Gungupa, Chamba, Nanumba North, Ghana. Please contact us to confirm directions before you travel.

Do I need to bring anything for a visit?

Farm visits can mean uneven paths and mud. We’ll share any helpful details when we confirm your visit.
